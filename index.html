About

I started in general web development and moved toward Linux systems, security and cloud work. What keeps me there is the practical side: finding weak permissions, reading auth logs, closing ports, and making software behave on a small machine or a free tier.

I work by building something, checking it against its own claims, and writing down what fell short. This site does the same: each case study separates what the code shows from what I have not verified.
